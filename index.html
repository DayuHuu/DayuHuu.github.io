About Me

Dayu Hu is a Ph.D. candidate supervised by Prof. Xinwang Liu at National University of Defense Technology. His research interests include graph neural network, bioinformatics and clustering. He has published 9 papers as first author including ACM MM, TFS, and BIB, with 232 citations and 8 h-index. He received awards such as two China National Scholarships, Excellent Master Thesis, etc. He also serves as reviewer for TKDE, TNNLS, TOMM, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, BIB, etc.
